# Editorial board

The editorial board is being assembled ahead of the journal's first issue.
Names and affiliations will be published here in full before the first call
for submissions closes.

We would rather show this page empty than fill it with names we have not
confirmed.

## What the board does

The board sets the journal's scope, appoints reviewers, and takes final
decisions on publication. Members serve in a named, public capacity: every
person listed here will appear with their institutional affiliation and their
area of specialism.
