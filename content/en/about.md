# About the journal

*Silk Road Economic Review* is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal of
economics published by the Economic Society of Uzbekistan.

## Aims and scope

We publish original empirical and theoretical research in economics, together
with policy notes, review articles, case studies and book reviews.

Our focus is the economies of Central Asia and the Caucasus. We welcome work
about the region, work that draws on data from the region, and comparative
research that places the region in a wider context. Submissions that use the
region only as an incidental setting are less likely to find a home here.

Subject areas include, without being limited to: macroeconomic policy and
central banking; trade, integration and connectivity; labour markets and
migration; agriculture, water and land; energy and natural resources; public
finance; industrial and firm-level analysis; development and inequality; and
economic history of the region.

## Languages

The journal publishes in English, Uzbek and Russian, and treats all three as
languages of record rather than as translations of a primary edition. An
article's full text may be in any of them.

Metadata is held separately for each language, and an article may be published
with only one of the three completed. Where a translation does not exist, the
page says so plainly and shows the text in a language that does.

Uzbek is set in Latin script.

## Open access

Every article is free to read from the day it is published, with no embargo,
no registration and no paywall.

Articles are published under a [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Authors retain
copyright in their own work. Anyone may read, download, copy, distribute,
print, search or link to the full text, provided the authors are credited.

**There are no fees of any kind.** No submission charge, no article processing
charge, no page charge, no colour charge. Publication is free for authors and
reading is free for everyone.

## Permanence

Once an article is published its address does not change. Article URLs and PDF
links are permanent, and corrections are published as separate records linked
to the original rather than by editing a file in place.
