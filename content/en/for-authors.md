# For authors

## How to propose an article

We accept proposals year-round. Send a title and an abstract through the
[proposal form](/en/submit); an editor reads every proposal and replies
directly. You may propose in English, Uzbek or Russian.

There is no manuscript system to register with and no account to create. If an
editor invites a full manuscript, the rest of the correspondence happens by
email.

## What we publish

Research articles, review articles, case studies, policy notes, editorials and
book reviews. See [about the journal](/en/about) for the scope.

The article of record is the PDF. We publish metadata and host the file; we do
not re-typeset submissions into web pages.

## Languages

You may publish in English, Uzbek or Russian. We encourage authors to supply
title, abstract and keywords in more than one language where they can, because
it makes the work findable to more of the region — but an article is never
delayed or refused for having only one.

Uzbek is set in Latin script.

## Publication ethics

The journal follows the principles of the [Committee on Publication Ethics
(COPE)](https://publicationethics.org/).

**Authorship.** Everyone listed as an author must have contributed
substantially to the work and must have approved the submitted version.
Anyone who contributed but does not meet that bar should be acknowledged
rather than listed.

**Originality.** Submissions must be original, not under consideration
elsewhere, and not previously published. Reusing your own earlier text without
citation is not acceptable.

**Conflicts of interest.** Authors must disclose funding sources and any
financial, institutional or personal relationship that a reader could
reasonably think had influenced the work. Disclosure is not disqualifying;
concealment is.

**Data and methods.** Authors should be able to supply the data and code
behind their results on reasonable request, except where confidentiality or
licensing genuinely prevents it.

**Corrections.** Errors that affect the interpretation of results are
published as a correction linked to the original article. The original is
never silently edited and never removed.

## Licence and copyright

Authors retain copyright. Articles are published under [CC BY
4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which lets anyone share
and adapt the work provided the authors are credited.

## Fees

There are none, in either direction.
